## Withheld original figures: purpose and missing evidence

- **Original Figures 1–2, loss versus wall time for Ex1–7:** intended to compare optimization progress and speed. Current histories preserve progress information, but four-GPU concurrent accuracy times do not provide isolated comparative timing. Old execution/timing scopes are not authenticated. Removed from active evidentiary displays for **missing evidence**; results tables do not claim to answer runtime efficiency. No timing campaign was commissioned.
- **Original Figure 3, Ex8 loss/RMSE versus time:** intended to show both training dynamics and recovery speed. Final selected models and noisy histories are available, but not a validated all-iteration true-error trajectory or isolated timing. Final 30-seed distributions answer final recovery only. No intermediate errors were inferred from final checkpoints.
- **Original Figures 4–5, Ex8 true/learned fields:** original purpose retained, now corrected-data seed-0 models on the fixed grid. These are direct replacements for coefficient visualization, with raw Sigma labels rather than ambiguous factor/covariance labels.
- **Original Figure 6, trajectory histograms:** intended to test learned long-time dynamics/distributions. No corrected authenticated trajectories or full simulation/SPD/boundary protocol exists. Withheld for **missing evidence**; coefficient/NLL panels do not establish trajectory fidelity. New simulation was not performed.
- **Original Figures 7–9, scaling:** intended to demonstrate N/h/K rates with historical modified-Ex7 and excess NLL. The NLL-infinity/execution chain is incomplete and the rate claims unsupported. Completed Ex8 sensitivity appears in the same subsection structure but is labelled different descriptive evidence, not a recovered historical replication.
- Original compilation-time table: measurement/version chain does not justify reuse for corrected current production. Withheld for **missing comparable evidence**, not replaced by a speed claim.

## Established corrections versus unexplained differences

Established: OOF targets, operational SPD handling and raw covariance reporting; fixed-lag SSA hazards/observations; Ex6 label/effective-noise convention; Ex8 float64 integration; actual capacity matching and evaluation populations. These have evidence in the prior reconciliation and source manifests.

Still not uniquely explained: historical→current coefficient discrepancies in Ex1/2/6; the exact cause of Ex3 poor repeated shallow fits; historical Ex7 NLL convention/execution differences; incomplete historical scaling and trajectory chains. This revision does not infer that every change is an algorithmic improvement or that one correction explains every difference. The corrected source metrics are retained without forcing agreement with old values.
