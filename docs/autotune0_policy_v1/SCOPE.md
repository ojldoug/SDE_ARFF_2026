# Scope and documentation source

Official source consulted: https://openxla.org/xla/determinism (22 September 2026). It distinguishes compilation-time autotuning variability from execution-time nondeterministic operations. The candidate disables autotuning; no second flag was used. Potential performance tradeoffs and the absence of a general determinism guarantee remain explicit.

This is a prospective execution-policy verification, not a historical-cause proof or a scientific estimator comparison. Three minimal processes and, only after their exact gate passed, two complete fits are authorized. The policy remains opt-in; numerical runners, production defaults, accepted artifacts and manuscripts are frozen.

The prior failed comparison at rtol=1e-5, atol=1e-6 is preserved. A different repeatable result cannot replace that failure or the paper's results. Data generation, other estimators, other seeds/configurations, other GPUs/hardware/software versions, and statistical distributional reproduction are outside scope.
